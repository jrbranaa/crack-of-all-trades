A Message From Your Handyman

Behind the Name

It started with me. Turns out I'm a polymath — genuinely good at carpentry, plumbing, painting, wiring, small appliance repair, and, apparently, exposing my lower back to the neighborhood no matter what I'm doing. Bending down. Standing up. Simply existing near a tool belt. It happens.

Then my sons Gavin and Parker started working alongside me, and it turns out the trait runs in the family. All three of us. Every job. I've made peace with it. Our chiropractor is doing great business.

What you actually get: three sets of hands, real skill across five trades, fair pricing, and a crew that shows up on time and finishes the job right — with the kind of good humor you need when it's not just one guy bending over, but three of us, in a row, doing the exact same thing.
